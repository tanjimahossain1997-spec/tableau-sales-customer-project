# tableau-sales-customer-project
## Project Overview
This project uses Tableau to analyze sales and customer data through interactive dashboards. It highlights key metrics such as sales, profit, customer behavior, and purchasing trends to identify top-performing segments, uncover business insights, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project consists of two interactive dashboards:
- Sales Dashboard
- Customer Dashboard

# Sales Dashboard

## Objective

The Sales Dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance, enabling users to analyze year-over-year sales trends, monitor key performance indicators, compare product performance, and identify weekly sales and profit patterns. The dashboard is designed to support strategic business decisions through dynamic and interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Overview

Displays key business metrics for both the current and previous year, including:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold

These KPI cards provide a quick comparison of overall business performance.

### Monthly Sales Trends

- Visualizes monthly Sales, Profit, and Quantity.
- Compares current year performance with the previous year.
- Highlights months with the highest sales performance.

### Product Sub-Category Analysis

- Compares sales across different product sub-categories.
- Displays Sales and Profit together to evaluate product profitability.
- Helps identify best-performing and underperforming products.

### Weekly Sales & Profit Analysis

- Displays weekly Sales and Profit for the selected year.
- Shows average weekly performance.
- Highlights weeks performing above and below the average.

### Interactive Features

- Dynamic year selection
- Interactive dashboard filtering
- Filter by Category
- Filter by Sub-Category
- Filter by Region
- Filter by State
- Filter by City
- Navigation between Sales and Customer dashboards

## Business Value

The dashboard enables businesses to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Compare year-over-year growth
- Identify seasonal trends
- Evaluate product profitability
- Analyze regional performance
- Support strategic sales decisions

# 👥 Customer Dashboard

## Objective

The Customer Dashboard provides an interactive overview of customer behavior and purchasing trends. It helps businesses understand customer engagement, identify high-value customers, evaluate purchasing patterns, and improve customer relationship strategies through data-driven insights.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Overview

Displays customer-related metrics for both the current and previous year:

- Total Customers
- Sales per Customer
- Total Orders

### Customer Trends

- Monthly customer trends
- Monthly sales per customer
- Monthly order trends
- Year-over-year comparison
- Highlights highest and lowest performing months

### Customer Distribution

Analyzes customer purchasing behavior based on the number of orders placed.

Provides insights into:

- Customer engagement
- Customer loyalty
- Purchase frequency
- Repeat customers

### Top 10 Customers by Profit

Displays the highest profit-generating customers along with:

- Customer Rank
- Number of Orders
- Sales
- Profit
- Last Order Date

### Interactive Features

- Dynamic year selection
- Interactive dashboard filters
- Easy navigation between dashboards
- Cross-filtering between visualizations

## Business Value

The dashboard helps organizations:

- Identify high-value customers
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Improve customer retention strategies
- Analyze customer profitability
- Support marketing and customer relationship decisions

# How to Use

1. Open the Tableau Public dashboard using the link above.
2. Select the desired year to compare historical performance.
3. Use filters to explore data by Category, Sub-Category, Region, State, and City.
4. Click charts to interactively filter related visualizations.
5. Navigate between the Sales and Customer dashboards to explore different business perspectives.
